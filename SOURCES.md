# Sources

Every fact in the app (`js/data.js`) and where it comes from.

## The float: Niwatori Hoko 鶏鉾

The main source is the museum panel supplied for this project
(`assets/panel-reference-jp.webp`, panel no. 17, "鶏鉾 〈にわとりほこ〉").
Translated:

> **Highlight: a back tapestry showing Greek myth.**
>
> - **Front hanging (前懸):** an early-18th-century Persian (Safavid) carpet with an
>   arabesque flower design, called a Herat carpet.
> - **Side hangings (胴懸):** embroidered copies of two votive pictures (ema) at
>   Kiyomizu-dera, the *Red-seal ship of Suminokura* (right) and the *Red-seal ship of
>   Sumiyoshi* (left), made new in 1980 (Showa 55). There is also an Indian carpet with
>   a "flowers and grasses" design.
> - **Mizuhiki (水引, the band under the eaves):** the top band is embroidered with
>   auspicious clouds, the sun, two kirin at the sides and a phoenix at the back. The
>   lower band, *Figures of the Tang Palace*, was drawn by Goshun and completed by
>   Ganku. The second band, *Butterflies of Spring and Autumn*, embroiders 50
>   butterflies from a drawing by Goshun's pupil Matsumura Keibun. A third band of fruit
>   and baskets is said to be from a drawing by Maruyama Ōkyo.
> - **Back tapestry (見送):** a Belgian Gobelin weave showing a scene from the Greek
>   epic the *Iliad*, "Hector setting out for the Trojan War and parting from his wife
>   and child". The design is said to be by Raphael. It is one of the oldest of its kind
>   still in Japan and, together with the one on Koi-yama, an Important Cultural
>   Property.
> - **Address:** Niwatoriboko-chō, Muromachi-dōri Shijō-sagaru, Shimogyō-ku, Kyoto.

The app uses: it is a float in the Gion Matsuri parade; its back tapestry was woven
in Belgium, shows a scene from the *Iliad*, and is one of the oldest of its kind in Japan.

## The festival: Gion Matsuri 祇園祭

- Kyoto's summer festival of Yasaka Shrine, running through the whole of July, with the
  Yamahoko float processions on 17 and 24 July. It began in 869 as a rite against
  epidemics, so it is more than 1,100 years old.
  - [UNESCO: Yamahoko, the float ceremony of the Kyoto Gion festival](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/yamahoko-the-float-ceremony-of-the-kyoto-gion-festival-00276)
  - [Yasaka Shrine: Gion Matsuri](https://www.yasaka-jinja.or.jp/en/)
